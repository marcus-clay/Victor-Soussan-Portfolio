# **The Deep Dive: Building Fluidity at Scale**

## **1\. Defining the Core Problem: The Contextual Shift**

In 2014, PagesJaunes was a robust, utility-first product, but its digital presence was rooted in static web directory thinking. The mobile app, despite having millions of downloads, felt heavy and required a significant leap in interaction design and visual language to meet modern mobile standards. Our mandate was clear: transform a functional legacy product into a fluid, personal, and cohesive cross-platform experience.  
**\[Visual Cue 1: The Before & After\]**

* **Visual Type:** Split screen, Before/After image.  
* **Content:** The old "Who / Where" search homepage (Before) contrasted with the new conversational homepage design (After, featuring a contextual background image and the friendly greeting: *"Bonjour, de quoi avez-vous besoin ?"*).  
* **Style Note:** Show both versions within premium device mockups (iPhone/Android phone) on a clean, neutral background.

## **2\. Reimagining the First Touch: Conversational Search & Onboarding**

### **A. The Intent-First Homepage**

We moved away from presenting two input fields (Who & Where) towards a singular, intent-driven search experience. The redesigned homepage became less about *listings* and more about *assistance*.

* **Conversational Tone:** Introducing a warm welcome to set a friendly tone.  
* **Visual Context:** We designed an art direction system for high-quality, rotating background images of local professionals (bakers, florists, mechanics), chosen based on the user's geo-location or time of day. This brought a human element to the utility search.

### **B. Engineering Seamless Onboarding**

With the launch of a major app version (v8.0), we needed to guide millions of existing users and new sign-ups through the updated experience without friction. This work included designing and delivering the new user onboarding experience across iOS and Android.

* **Custom Motion:** We designed short, non-blocking animations for the first launch. On iOS, we leveraged CAAnimation for smooth walkthroughs. On Android, we used subtle Material Design cues and Activity transitions.  
* **Measured Impact:** These animations were crucial for feature discovery (Favorites, Maps) and led to a measurable uplift in first-week retention.

**\[Visual Cue 2: Motion Design Showcase\]**

* **Visual Type:** Short, high-quality GIF or MP4 loop.  
* **Content:** A side-by-side comparison showing the fluid transition of the new onboarding flow on both iOS and Android (onboardingiosandroid.mp4).  
* **Style Note:** Focus on the *smoothness* of the transition, using a clean gradient background.

## **3\. Core System Refinement: Consistency and Loyalty**

A high-traffic utility product requires rock-solid core flows. We undertook a deep revision of critical user actions, ensuring simplicity and robust cross-platform parity.

### **A. Subscription and Login Overhaul**

The existing login and subscription flow was fragmented. We modularized the components and streamlined the steps to reduce user confusion and increase successful sign-ins/sign-ups.  
**\[Visual Cue 3: Flow & Interface Detail\]**

* **Visual Type:** Composite image/flow diagram.  
* **Content:** A focused view showing the new, clean, modularized login/registration interface, highlighting the simplified steps (loginredesign.png).  
* **Style Note:** Display mockups within a neutral browser/device frame to emphasize the clarity of the new UI.

### **B. Driving Retention with "My PagesJaunes"**

As UX Lead on Core User Flows, I led the redesign of the loyalty and account management areas. The goal was to support the user retention strategy by making it easier to save and retrieve information.

* **User Profiling:** Designed the full "My PagesJaunes" experience, focusing on favoriting, saving, user history, and account creation/management.  
* **Contribution:** Also designed the full experience for photo and review contributions from the business profile pages, turning passive users into active contributors.

**\[Visual Cue 4: Loyalty & Account Screens\]**

* **Visual Type:** Grid of high-fidelity screens.  
* **Content:** A 4-up grid showcasing the new "My PagesJaunes" tab, the redesigned Favorites list, the Recent Activity view, and the Account settings screen.  
* **Style Note:** A clean arrangement of screens with slightly rounded corners and minimal chrome.

### **C. Establishing Design Foundations (Strategy Focus)**

To combat feature divergence, I conducted the initial audit and strategy for a cross-platform component system ("Yellowstrap").

* **Strategic Audit:** Performed a comprehensive audit of all existing app screens and internal user research across digital squads (designers and developers) to define the specific needs for a design system.  
* **Roadmap Delivery:** Delivered a strategic recommendation document and guidelines initiative for the native app component system, which became the foundational brief for subsequent team members to build the library. **My role focused on the strategic definition and requirements, not the hands-on building.**

## **4\. Real-World Utility: Engineering the Navigation Experience**

One of the most critical aspects of local search is fast, accurate navigation. We fundamentally enhanced the map and routing experience to make finding and getting to a professional faster and more reliable.

### **A. Geospatial Clarity and Performance**

We integrated with the Mappy API to build our own directions UI on top, ensuring core utility even on slower networks (3G).

* **Multi-App Handoff:** Designed clear options allowing users to seamlessly hand off navigation to their preferred external app (Waze, Google Maps, Apple Maps) while maintaining context within the PagesJaunes ecosystem.  
* **Pedestrian Routes:** Created the new **“Walking Itinerary”** experience, which provided robust, turn-by-turn pedestrian route previews directly from a business listing to the destination.

**\[Visual Cue 5: Map Flow and Pedestrian Route\]**

* **Visual Type:** Animated GIF or split screen showcasing two core functions.  
* **Content:** A transition showing the flow from a business listing, selecting "Walking Itinerary," and the map focusing on the pedestrian route line and steps (walkingitinerary.gif or mapflow.mp4).  
* **Style Note:** Use a mobile device mockup (iPhone or Android) with a high contrast map view.

### **B. Responsive Map Design for Tablet**

The map experience was completely redesigned for the iPad, utilizing native iOS split views to support a more powerful and contextual workflow in landscape orientation.

* **Split Views:** Emphasized route clarity by separating the directions list from the interactive map view in a persistent, side-by-side layout.  
* **Accessibility:** Ensured full support for accessibility features like Dynamic Type and VoiceOver, making the utility product available to all users.

**\[Visual Cue 6: Tablet Map View\]**

* **Visual Type:** Horizontal/landscape mockup.  
* **Content:** The iPad directions view showing the map on one side and the step-by-step instructions/contextual cards on the other (ipadsplitview.png).  
* **Style Note:** Display the design within a premium iPad Pro mockup on a clean background.

## **5\. Innovation, Impact, and Legacy**

The two-year engagement successfully repositioned PagesJaunes' core products for a mobile-centric future. The systemic changes resulted in both tangible business metrics and foundational improvements in how the product was built.

### **Key Metrics Achieved:**

* **App Scale:** Reached over **22 Million cumulative downloads.**  
* **Engagement Uplift:** **\+18% increase in homepage search Click-Through Rate (CTR).**  
* **Operational Efficiency:** 40% reduction in UI handoff issues reported by dev teams.  
* **User Satisfaction:** App Store ratings rose from an average of 4.2 to 4.5.

### **Strategic Legacy**

The work on core flows (login, onboarding, account management) and the strategic audit for "Yellowstrap" established a consistent foundation, supporting long-term feature scalability and maintaining visual integrity across one of France’s most popular B2C services. Furthermore, our exploration into the Renault R-Link application and the Android Wear app positioned the brand as a forward-thinking utility in emerging platforms.

### **What I Learned**

Designing for legacy users at this scale taught me the critical balance between radical vision and pragmatic, incremental constraints. The biggest lesson was the value of **design strategy over pure production**—my focus shifted from simply building UIs to defining the *structure and rules* (Design System audit) and tackling **complex utility challenges** (map routing, multi-app handoff) that impact millions daily. This experience solidified my approach to leading design systems and driving measurable retention through core flow precision.
