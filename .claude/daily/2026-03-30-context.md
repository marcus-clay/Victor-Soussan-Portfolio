Victor is about to start a CDI (permanent position). The portfolio site must now generate inbound for:
- Speaking engagements (conferences, corporate talks)
- Brownbag lunches at companies
- Training and workshops on AI + Design (Claude Code, Figma MCP, prototyping)
- The "Designer Augmenté par l'IA" positioning
- Freelance consulting as secondary (side activity)

**Why:** The monetization model shifts from selling design missions to selling expertise transfer (talks, training, workshops). The site should position Victor as the go-to expert on AI-augmented design, not as a freelance designer looking for projects.

**How to apply:** Hero, CTAs, and site narrative must signal thought leadership and availability for speaking/training, not availability for hire. "Frame. Design. Ship." and "Lead Product Designer end-to-end" are misaligned with this new direction.

**LinkedIn content strategy:** Victor actively publishes tutorials on Figma + Claude, Claude Code usage, AI-augmented design practice, and how it redefines product design processes. The site (especially the hero) must serve as a landing page for LinkedIn traffic: visitors arrive from a specific post and need immediate coherence + a conversion path toward booking a talk/training.
