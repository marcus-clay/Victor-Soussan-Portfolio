## Article tone guidelines (from Victor)

MOFU/TOFU/BOFU model. Posture: Clay Christensen (JTBD), Luke Wroblewski (mobile-first), Sundar Pichai (clarity at scale), Emmanuel Macron (French public service context), Victor himself (practitioner). Tone: mature, professional, no jargon, no bullshit, no superiority, inspirational transmission, executive, well-articulated, solid education/digital France context, natural communication, clear product design rationale, qualitative data (no invented quanti), impact on efficiency/ease/gain. No LLM tonality, no "Ce n'est pas X, c'est Y", no staccato sentences, no passive-aggressive, peer-to-peer, human, authentic.

**Why:** Victor's readers are peers in his professional network. The content must position him as a thoughtful practitioner sharing field experience, not as someone performing expertise.

**How to apply:** Use these guidelines for all future article expansions and new content creation.
