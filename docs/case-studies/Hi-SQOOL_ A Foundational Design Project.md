# **Hi-SQOOL Dashboard (2019)**

## **Designing the foundational modular web experience that defined the digital future for the French Lycée Numérique program.**

### **30-Second Summary**

Faced with a constrained legacy system, we designed and prototyped the first web-based, modular student platform for high schools. This high-stakes Proof of Concept (PoC) consolidated critical resources—cloud storage, lesson sessions, and authentication—and successfully validated a *web-first* strategy on school hardware. More importantly, it established the indispensable design language and component foundation for the entire future **SQOOL Design System** and application suite.

## **I. Context & The Strategic Challenge**

### **Moving Beyond Constraint: A Shift to Web-First**

Following the ambitious launch of the national digital high school plan, our client required a digital interface capable of meeting the sophistication expected by a high school audience. The existing tablet launcher was rigid, visually ill-suited, and fundamentally limited the product's evolution.  
Our mandate was to **break free from the native constraints** and define a web-first user experience. The new platform, Hi-SQOOL, had to be modular, responsive, and capable of unifying critical tools—cloud storage access, dynamic lesson-sharing sessions, and application access—into one clean, unified dashboard.  
**The Problem:** The complexity of integrating various external educational services risked overwhelming users.  
**The Key Insight:** Through early prototyping, we strategically narrowed the scope. The final product needed to be **Simple, Fast, and Focused**. *Simplicity beats exhaustiveness.* We committed to perfecting the three core functions: **Authentication, Sessions, and Cloud Storage**.

## **II. Approach & System Foundation**

### **Designing for Architectural Longevity**

We framed the Hi-SQOOL project as a critical Proof of Concept (PoC) to establish the design language and technical feasibility for the organization. Our design approach was rooted in creating a robust, reusable component and grid system.  
**The Design Sprint:** We ran a tight 6-week design sprint focused on defining user goals for both teacher and student personas.

1. **Information Architecture (IA):** We mapped user journeys, creating clear access points for creation (Teacher) and consumption (Student).  
2. **Modular Component Definition:** We established the first set of UI patterns—typography, color palette, consistent card-based modules—which ensured scalability and predictability across different screen sizes.

This project was where the **SQOOL Design System** was informally born, setting the standards for visual design and interaction patterns that would be formally adopted and scaled across the entire product line in subsequent years.

## **III. Execution: Crafting Critical Journeys**

The success of Hi-SQOOL hinged on transforming complex, institutional processes into high-fidelity, intuitive digital interactions.

### **1\. The Secure Gateway: Modern Authentication**

We designed a new, embedded school-based login system that felt integrated and secure. The flow was streamlined to handle institutional credentials while introducing the mandatory **Acceptance of Charter** screen clearly and minimally. This was essential for reducing support tickets related to complex legacy access methods.

### **2\. The Collaborative Core: Pedagogical Sessions**

The Sessions functionality—where teachers distribute and manage lesson materials in real-time—was the functional heart of the platform. We created distinct, but visually consistent, UIs for both roles:

* **Teacher View:** A focused management interface for starting sessions and monitoring student access.  
* **Student View:** A simplified, non-distracting entry point for joining sessions and accessing materials.

### **3\. Immediate Access: Cloud Storage**

To ensure students could easily access their educational content, we integrated a light, dedicated **File Browser** module. This feature allowed students to navigate cloud storage directly from the dashboard, minimizing friction caused by external navigation or fragmented systems.

## **IV. Impact & Legacy**

### **A Strategic Success: The Foundation is Set**

Hi-SQOOL was successfully deployed in high schools in late 2019\. The project’s impact transcended the features themselves, proving a critical strategic point for the organization:

* **Web Strategy Validated:** It confirmed that complex educational UX could be delivered with a "native-like" feel via the web, freeing the product from a critical dependency on the legacy Android launcher.  
* **Precursor to the Suite:** The modular architecture and the core set of visual components defined in this sprint became the non-negotiable standard. The dashboard concept directly evolved into the flagship **SQOOL Connect** and allowed for the rapid, scalable development of the entire **SQOOL Apps Suite** in the years that followed (2020-2024).

The Hi-SQOOL PoC was the crucial starting line—it defined the future design language and architectural strategy, allowing the company to scale its product offering dramatically.  
**Key Learning:** *For a busy student audience, providing fewer, clearer entry points is vastly more effective than offering exhaustive features.*
