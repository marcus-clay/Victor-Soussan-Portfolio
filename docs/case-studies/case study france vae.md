    La matrice inclue les différentes caractéristiques du secteur de France VAE. Elle est pragmatique et permet aux équipes de prioriser les sujets de la feuille de route de manière efficace et éclairée.
    
    Pour chaque sujet de la feuille de route, nous établissons un score de pondération, construit à partir de plusieurs critères.
    
- **Qui remplit la matrice ?**
    
    Représentant de l’équipe Produit, représentant de l’équipe Métier, représentant de l’équipe Technique, représentant équipe Support, représentant équipe Design.
    
- **Comment remplir la matrice ?**
    
    Pour chaque critère, attribuer une note de 1 à 5, en fonction de la valeur du critère.

"I helped France VAE, the national platform for Validation of Prior Experience, turn an ambitious public initiative into actionable product experiences.

Key contributions:

**Product Strategy & Workflow Redesign**
- Co-designed with the Lead PM a new organizational model shifting from siloed teams (Product, Tech, Design, Support working separately) to unified contributors around common objectives
- Introduced a three-tier strategic framework: Objectives (the "why") → Initiatives (the "what") → Features (the "how"), with clear distinction between uncertain strategic goals and precise deliverables
- Established monthly "Seasons" with dedicated phases: Preparation (25% - needs gathering, problem analysis, cross-team prioritization), Execution (50% - 2 sprints with incremental deliveries), and Retrospective (25% - evaluation, KPI measurement, next season prep)
- Implemented parallel Discovery/Delivery tracks: Discovery to reduce uncertainty through investigation and experimentation, Delivery to ship validated solutions fast
- Created a cross-team prioritization matrix involving Product, Business, Design, Support, and Tech representatives, with weighted scoring criteria (user value, time criticality, business score, technical effort, specificity degree)
- Facilitated weekly sync meetings with PO and designers to plan discovery and delivery topics

**Collective VAE & Employer Journey**
- Designed the complete VAE Collective MVP: end-to-end employer journey for companies running collective certification programs
- Created B2B pitch decks for employer acquisition and onboarding preparation
- Produced a promotional video (screencast, scenario, script, editing, voice-over) showcasing the full candidate journey on France VAE—from diploma search to certification success, including all interactions between candidate, accompaniment structure, and certifier

**Platform Experience Redesign**
- Redesigned the institutional homepage of the public service
- Redesigned the candidate search engine experience to improve certification discovery
- Redesigned Google Slides brand templates for France VAE

**Accompagnateur Experience Exploration**
- Led a full exploration of the Accompagnateur experience, including audit and classification of all notification emails
- Categorized all system emails sent to users, audited them, and proposed improvements to reduce email volume and better target communication (transactional emails vs. important activity notifications)
- Facilitated problem definition and ideation workshops during team seminars (focused on notification system evolution)
- Facilitated a Crazy-8 workshop with the internal team
- Organized and facilitated a 2-day design-thinking workshop with field actors (influential accompaniment structures - AAP - on France VAE) to understand their work context, identify pain points with the service, and co-create solutions through ideation and sketching

**AI-Assisted Candidate Orientation (Experimentation Phase)**

Context: Candidates often struggle with orientation—55% don't know if VAE is right for them, and many choose certifications by default. The initiative explored how AI could help candidates identify if VAE fits their profile and which certification matches their skills.

- Led a prospective study on AI opportunities for candidate orientation, identifying user archetypes (experienced professionals without diplomas, career changers, job seekers in transition) and their specific barriers
- Ideated 10+ concept solutions for both engaged and non-engaged candidates: chatbots, skills-certification matching, career impact simulators, testimonial galleries, local job opportunity analyzers
- Designed and developed 2 functional prototypes to test different approaches:
  - **VAE Positioning Chatbot** ([live demo](https://joyful-unicorn-489a7b.netlify.app)): A conversational assistant to evaluate VAE relevance based on the candidate's professional situation
  - **AI-Assisted Professional Orientation with Skills Radar** ([live demo](https://flourishing-cascaron-0d6509.netlify.app)): An AI-powered tool that analyzes skills via CV/LinkedIn and guides candidates toward matching careers in the medical-social sector, with a visual radar chart showing skill gaps vs. certification requirements
- Built working prototypes to gather early feedback and validate concepts before further investment
- Note: This initiative was deprioritized but served as a valuable rapid prototyping exercise

**User Research & Testing**
- Structured the user research knowledge base and established weekly team rituals (design + business teams) to identify and plan research initiatives
- Designed and conducted 2 interview campaigns (10 total interviews across 2 waves) for the new candidate dashboard launch
- Presented 2 synthesis reports during team all-hands meetings
- Created consolidated feedback tracking (CSV) for ongoing analysis

**Design & Delivery Structuring**
- Proposed a new Figma file architecture to facilitate journey-based file organization
- Improved prototype signposting with start screens for better navigation
- Introduced lot-based prototyping: breaking designs into small batches easier to connect to dev user stories
- Pushed for designers to level up on prototyping skills and communicate through focused user journeys and nominal cases
- Specified UX/UI improvements through design QA tickets

**Brand & Communications**
- Drafted the first Product Vision documentation to frame discussions and guide prioritization
- Revamped brand identity and Google Slides master templates for unified internal and external communications

Impact: delivered a clear MVP, a solid foundation for future communication redesign, and faster, more aligned product decisions for a public service scaling nationwide."
