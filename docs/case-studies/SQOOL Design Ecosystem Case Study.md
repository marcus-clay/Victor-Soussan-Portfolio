# **Scaling Digital Education: Crafting the SQOOL Ecosystem**

## **From Single Product to Modular Suite: Defining a Future-Proof Experience for UNOWHY**

SQOOL is a comprehensive digital education solution, designed to provide seamless learning and management tools for students, teachers, and administrators across France. Our mandate was to evolve the initial product into a scalable, cohesive ecosystem capable of supporting millions of users and future app expansion.

### **Project Metadata**

| Role | Timeline | Context | Challenge | Outcome |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Product Design Leadership** (Strategy, UX/UI, System, Brand Identity) | 2019 – 2024 (5 Years) | Evolving a pilot platform (Hi-SQOOL) into a mature, modular suite of professional and educational applications. | Establish a unified design language, build a robust Figma Design System from scratch, and formalize product design rituals to support accelerated development and a growing product portfolio. | Successful launch of the **SQOOL Apps Suite** (Classe, Partage, MDM, Protect), full brand identity finalization, implementation of Cloud PC UX, and adoption of scalable design-ops rituals. |

### **The 30-Second Summary**

SQOOL faced a critical challenge: their initial platform, Hi-SQOOL, was not built for the complexity of a national digital education deployment. Over five years, we partnered with UNOWHY to lead the transformation, transitioning the product from a monolithic structure to a cohesive, modular app suite. This involved an end-to-end design effort: defining a new visual identity, leading deep UX research (e.g., the 'Connect' prototype), building the centralized Figma Design System, and instilling product design governance. The result is a robust, user-centric ecosystem (including core apps like SQOOL Classe and SQOOL Protect) that is now scalable for the next generation of digital learning.

### **2\. Conceptualization & Brand Alignment (2021)**

With technical proof points validated, the focus shifted to the overall user experience and brand coherence. This required establishing a persistent, guiding interface layer.  
The **"La Bulle"** concept emerged—a persistent animated launcher designed to anchor the entire ecosystem and simplify navigation between services, moving away from a traditional desktop metaphor.

| Visual Required | Description & Intent |
| :---- | :---- |
| **VISUAL 3: Animated** | **"La Bulle" UI Exploration.** An animation of the persistent launcher, showing its subtle idle state, activation, and the smooth transition/expansion into the main app dashboard. This should feel playful and highly polished. |

Simultaneously, we drove the **Brand System Concept Phase**, defining logo proposals, color palettes, and grid rules. This effort ensured that the new design language was directly connected to the UNOWHY brand values before major development began.

### **3\. The Shift to Modular Scalability (2022)**

A pivotal strategic decision was made: the monolithic product was broken down into the **SQOOL Apps Suite** (Classe, Partage, MDM, Applications). This shift required immediate design scalability.  
To handle this complexity, we centralized all UI components, patterns, and guidelines:

* **Figma Design System:** Created the first comprehensive design libraries (colors, typography, components) to ensure consistency across the five new applications launched.  
* **Design-Ops Foundation:** Initiated the Notion knowledge base to document usage, governance, and contribution guidelines for the design system.

### **4\. Specialization, Governance, and Finalization (2023-2024)**

The final two years focused on specialized product delivery and cementing design quality across the organization.  
**New Specialized Apps:** We expanded the ecosystem to cover critical areas like security and extended desktop solutions:

* **SQOOL Protect:** Prototyped and shipped the mobile UX flows for device security and parental controls.  
* **SQOOL Extend:** Worked on the PRD and workshops for the complex Cloud PC UX flows with partners (Weytop).

### **Conclusion**

The SQOOL ecosystem project was a long-term engagement that transcended simple interface design. It was a strategic undertaking to define the foundational architecture, brand expression, and operational rituals required for a high-growth, mission-critical product suite. By embedding design leadership and establishing a robust design system, we transformed UNOWHY's offering into a scalable, industry-leading platform that is now prepared for its continued deployment and evolution.
